HEALTH INSURANCE POLICY — Section 3: Claims
All claims must be submitted within 90 days of service date.
Pre-authorization is required for surgeries above $5,000.
Mental health visits are covered up to 20 sessions per year.
Emergency room visits require notification within 48 hours.
LEAVE POLICY — Section 7: Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days sick leave per calendar year.
Medical certificate required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.
Unused sick leave cannot be carried forward to the next year.

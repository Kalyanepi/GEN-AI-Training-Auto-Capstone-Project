EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK — IT Security Policy
Passwords must be changed every 90 days and contain 12+ characters.
VPN is mandatory when accessing company resources remotely.
Reporting a security incident: email security@company.com within 1 hour.
Personal devices require MDM enrollment before accessing company email.
EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Travel expenses above $500 require prior manager approval.
Meal allowance is $75 per day during business travel.
Receipts must be submitted within 30 days via the expense portal.
